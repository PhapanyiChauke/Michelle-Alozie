# Michelle-Alozie
Winner Poster

I designed this to celebrate and a huge congratulations to my favourite player, Michelle Alozie, for winning big at the NUKSA Awards as Best Female Defender 2026.

This poster is a tribute to her amazing performance and dedication

## Concept
Type: Winner Poster / Athlete Profile Poster 
Colour: Green & White inspired by Nigeria flag, which she represents for her national women's football team.
Titles: Nigeria women's football team has won 10 titles and received 10 stars on their jersey, showing they are known as the strongest team in Africa.
